RESEARCH STRATEGY & METHODOLOGY (2-3 minutes)
---------------------------------------------
"Let me take a moment to explain my research approach, because understanding how I gather insights is crucial to understanding the quality of my recommendations.

[POINT TO RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY]
I believe in a mixed-methods approach that combines the depth of qualitative research with the breadth of quantitative data. This gives us both the 'why' and the 'what' - the emotional drivers and the behavioral patterns.

[SCROLL TO RESEARCH METHODS]
My research strategy follows a three-phase approach:

**Phase 1: Discovery & Exploration**
I start with broad exploratory research to understand the landscape. This includes:
- Competitive analysis to understand market positioning
- User reviews and feedback analysis to identify pain points
- Stakeholder interviews to understand business objectives
- Heuristic evaluations to assess current experience quality

**Phase 2: Deep-Dive Qualitative Research**
This is where I get into the human experience:
- One-on-one user interviews (typically 15-20 participants)
- Contextual inquiry sessions to observe real usage
- Journey mapping workshops with key stakeholders
- Emotional journey mapping to understand feelings at each touchpoint

**Phase 3: Validation & Quantification**
Finally, I validate insights with broader data:
- Surveys with larger sample sizes (100+ participants)
- Analytics data analysis to confirm patterns
- A/B testing to validate hypotheses
- Usability testing to refine solutions

[POINT TO RESEARCH RIGOR]
What makes my research approach effective is the rigor I apply to each phase. For example, in the QCC Connect project, I conducted 18 in-depth interviews with students across different demographics, analyzed over 200 user reviews, and mapped the complete emotional journey from initial awareness to daily usage.

[POINT TO INSIGHTS TRANSLATION]
But research is only valuable if it translates to action. I create clear research artifacts - journey maps, personas, opportunity matrices - that help teams understand not just what users want, but why they want it and how it impacts the business.

This methodology ensures that every recommendation I make is grounded in real user needs and validated through multiple research methods..."

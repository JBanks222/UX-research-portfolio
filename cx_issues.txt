Lack of Awareness of Campus Events

Students don’t know what events are happening, even though they’re listed in the app.

The Events page is underused despite being frequently updated.

Disjointed Access to Academic Support

Before Navigate360, students had to access multiple disconnected systems for help.

This created friction in finding advisors, scheduling appointments, or tracking progress.

Inconsistent or Interrupted Digital Service Delivery

Issues like the digital ID system going down disrupt student routines.

There is a need for real-time troubleshooting and system resilience.

Invisible, Friction-Filled Systems

Students often don’t realize they’re interacting with multiple services (e.g., ID scanning, scheduling).

The goal is to make those interactions seamless and intuitive.

Low Engagement With Support Tools

Students aren’t engaging with all the digital support tools available to them.

There’s a need for better onboarding, UX clarity, or proactive communication.

Digital to Physical Disconnect

One of the biggest goals is bridging the gap between the digital experience (apps, alerts) and real-world action (attending events, visiting support centers).

Volin’s vision is to guide students from information to in-person participation.
